Work in Progress

Personal brand systems currently in development. Tap a card for a brief.

Currus
Automotive Brand
+

Most car brands are quietly failing at EV sub-branding right now, either bolting on a green badge and calling it done, or launching a whole new identity that abandons everything customers already recognize. Currus Motors is built to answer that problem properly. One master logo, one restrained two-color system, and the EV mark is a deliberate evolution of the original, not a break from it. Three models, family, sport, SUV, exist in both fuel and electric versions, proving the same brand can flex across a full range without fragmenting into five different logos and a dozen inconsistent touchpoints. The full system gets built out across brand guidelines, a working website prototype, two brochures, social creative, a billboard, and an event mockup, everything a real launch would need. Car imagery is AI-generated, deliberately and openly, standing in for commissioned photography, this project is about proving brand systems thinking, not claiming a skill in rendering vehicles.

The Real Scoop
FMCG / Ice Cream Brand
+

Most "healthy" ice cream brands quietly hide the compromise, thin serving sizes disguised as normal ones, artificial sweeteners buried in the fine print, ingredient lists built to survive a glance, not a real read. The Real Scoop is built on the opposite bet, that honesty itself can be the premium positioning. Four lines sit under one masterbrand, Protein for genuine recovery, Balance for people managing sugar without wanting to feel punished for it, Plant-based that's actually creamy instead of icy, and Exotic for flavor territory most brands won't touch, plus Two Spoons, a sharing-occasion line built around flavor pairings people actually want to be seen buying. Every ingredient decision traces back to a real commitment, real dairy where it's dairy, no artificial sweeteners, nothing hidden. Even packaging follows the same logic, two honest cup sizes, no oversized tubs pretending to be a better deal. It's a full brand architecture, not a single product with a clever name.

Solidus
Finance / Credit Brand
+

Credit and personal finance branding tends to fall into one of two traps, cold and clinical, or aggressively friendly in a way that feels like it's hiding something. Solidus is an early exploration of a third way, a credit-based finance brand built around clarity and trust rather than either extreme. Still taking shape, more will land here as the concept develops.
